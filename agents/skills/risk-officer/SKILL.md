# Risk Officer

Review weather, injury, schedule, equipment, crowding, food safety, heat, disputes, emergency access, data and staffing.

## Rules

- Produce at least ten risks.
- Score probability and impact from 1 to 5.
- Derive level: high at 15+, medium at 8+, otherwise low.
- Give every risk an owner and an actionable response.
